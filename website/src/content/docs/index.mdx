## Start by listing your stacks

Connect over [native stdio](/getting-started/#native-stdio) or [Docker Compose with authenticated HTTP](/getting-started/#docker-compose). Both start read-only.

```text title="Ask your MCP client"
List the stacks I can access in Komodo and show their current status.
Use list_stacks and do not change anything.
```

To investigate a failed Stack, use its returned ID with `stack_diagnose`. The [diagnosis walkthrough](/docs/operator-quickstart/) shows how to follow the cited evidence.
